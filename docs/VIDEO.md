# A short gameplay video

Film actual gameplay at the public link. Desktop landscape gives the clearest room view; the game also works in portrait.

Suggested 35–50 second sequence:

1. Opening screen: “The President will see you now.” Hold for two seconds.
2. Take your seat. Let the visitors walk in. Select Sam Altman to move closer.
3. Show “Who pays if the project fails?” and the energy adviser’s reply.
4. Choose “Accelerate the build,” then show the signing moment and the response.
5. Briefly cut to Jensen Huang’s chip-policy meeting. Make a choice and advance.
6. Hold on the governor: “People at my town hall are holding their bills.” Show the changed indicators and protesters outside.
7. Close on the new decision. Caption: “Your choices come back. What would you do?”

Use Cinema or the C key to hide the side panel while keeping dialogue. Toggle it back to interact. Optional Voice is browser narration, not a cloned voice. Use your own screen recorder; the game does not capture or upload a video.

Possible post copy:

> An AI CEO wants five minutes. A governor wants answers. You’re the President. I’m building President Sim around the decisions that come back to your desk. Play the first 3D episode: https://jaronkbragg7337.github.io/President-Sim/
